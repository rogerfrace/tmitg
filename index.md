# the Machine in the Garden

> the Machine in the Garden is an independent gothic/etherealwave duo featuring Roger Fracé and Summer Bowman. Formed in 1992, the band continues to develop its atmospheric darkwave and ethereal gothic rock style.

## About

The Machine in the Garden is an independent gothic/etherealwave duo featuring Roger Fracé and Summer Bowman. Since their formation in the early 1990s, Roger and Summer have developed and advanced their unique style through years of collaborating and intertwining their musical tastes. The band has released eight full-length albums and one EP.

## Recent News

### New Single: Space-Time

Published 22 May 2026.

The Machine in the Garden's new single and first new track in 6 years, released on World Goth Day 2026. Keeping with the band's established yet evolving style, "Space-Time" builds slowly with patient, deliberate purpose: a harmonically rich, atmospheric track with a strong sense of emotional arc, Summer Bowman's voice tracing the space between stillness and release.

"Space-Time" is available as a [free download on Bandcamp](https://tmitg.bandcamp.com/track/space-time) and as a [lyric video on YouTube](https://youtu.be/iJ0sCc1bwBQ).
